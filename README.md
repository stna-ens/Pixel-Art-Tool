## 🎨 The Philosophy
I am a student who loves pixel art but hates modern software bloat.
I noticed a problem: Mobile apps are full of ads, they feel like a toy, and they have not so good interfaces that just demotivates you from the start. And desktop apps are too complex or expensive for casual users. As a result I wanted to build a simple, pretty looking, intuitive app that feels like actual software.

**Pixy Studio** is my answer.
It is built with a **"Zero-Dependency"** philosophy. No heavy frameworks, no tracking, no login walls. Just you and the grid.

## ⚡ Universal Experience
Pixy Studio isn't just a website; it's a responsive web application designed to adapt to your workflow, regardless of the device.

* **📱 On iPad & Tablets:** A touch-first interface that feels native. Supports multi-touch gestures and palm rejection logic.
* **💻 On Desktop:** A precise studio environment with full mouse support and keyboard shortcuts for rapid workflow.
* **🤳 On Mobile:** A streamlined pocket edition for sketching assets on the go.

## 🛠 Core Features
Designed to be minimal yet powerful enough for game development and digital art.

### 🔹 Layer Management System
Don't limit yourself to one canvas.
* Create multiple layers to separate outlines, colors, and shading.
* Toggle visibility, merge layers, or rearrange them with ease.

### 🔹 Precision Drawing Engine
* **Pixel-Perfect Pen:** Draws exactly where you touch without aliasing.
* **Smart Eraser:** Clean up your sprites instantly.
* **Dynamic Grid:** Helps you maintain perspective and proportions.

### 🔹 Color Control
* **Custom Themes:** Build your own color schemes.
* **History:** Quickly access recently used colors.
* **Eyedropper:** Sample colors directly from your canvas.

### 🔹 Production Ready
* **Offline Mode (PWA):** Install it to your home screen. It works 100% offline via Service Workers.
* **Local Storage:** Your artwork is saved automatically to your device's browser storage.
* **Export:** Download your work as high-quality PNGs with transparent backgrounds, ready for game engines.

## 🏗 Under the Hood
This project is an exploration of the **HTML5 Canvas API** capabilities.

* **Core:** Vanilla JavaScript (ES6 Modules)
* **Rendering:** 2D Canvas Context optimization
* **State Management:** Custom observable pattern (No Redux/Signals)
* **Styling:** CSS Variables & Flexbox
* **Build:** Vite
